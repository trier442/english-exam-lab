문단 지도와 문항별 근거 나누기

긴 지문은 문단 역할을 먼저 표시하고, 전체 요약과 세부 추론을 서로 다른 근거 범위에서 해결하세요.

2문항
PASSAGE

When Efficiency Creates Fragility

Systems are often improved by removing unused capacity. A delivery company may reduce the number of spare vehicles, or a hospital may schedule staff so that almost every hour is occupied. Under normal conditions, these changes can lower costs and appear highly efficient.

However, spare capacity has another function: it absorbs variation. If several vehicles fail on the same day or patient numbers suddenly rise, a system operating near its maximum has little room to respond. What looked like waste during ordinary periods can become protection during unusual ones.

This does not mean that every organization should maintain large reserves. Excess capacity also has costs. The important question is how much variability the system faces and how expensive failure would be. Efficiency should therefore be judged together with resilience, because a system optimized only for average conditions may perform poorly precisely when conditions stop being average.

글은 효율성을 부정하지 않으면서, 변동과 실패 비용을 고려해 여유 용량과 함께 평가해야 한다고 주장합니다. 1·4번처럼 ‘항상 최대한 확보’하는 극단적 주장은 아닙니다.
