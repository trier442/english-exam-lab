5문항
PASSAGE 1

Confidence Needs Calibration

People often judge the quality of a prediction by whether it turns out to be correct. But a single correct prediction can result from luck. A more useful test asks whether confidence matches actual accuracy across many predictions. If someone says “I am 80 percent sure” repeatedly, about eight out of ten such predictions should be correct. This relationship is called calibration. Good calibration does not require certainty; it requires that the strength of confidence reflect the quality of available evidence. A person who is appropriately uncertain may therefore be making better judgments than someone who sounds decisive but is frequently wrong.

PASSAGE 2

More Choice, More Search

Online stores can display thousands of products at almost no physical cost. Yet a larger catalog does not automatically make shopping easier. Each additional option creates another possible comparison, and comparisons require attention. Filters, default settings, and useful categories are valuable because they reduce the number of options that must be considered at once. Their purpose is not to eliminate choice, but to ________. A well-designed store helps customers reach a manageable set of relevant options before asking them to compare details.

선택지를 고른 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

정답 2번

필터와 범주는 선택 자체를 없애는 것이 아니라 한 번에 비교해야 하는 대상을 줄여 탐색의 인지 비용을 낮춥니다.

PASSAGE 3

Variation Is Information

When a process produces different results from day to day, managers may be tempted to treat every difference as a problem. But variation can have several sources. Some fluctuation is random and temporary; other changes signal that the process itself has shifted. If every small change is immediately corrected, managers may end up adding noise rather than removing it. A better approach is to collect enough observations to distinguish ordinary fluctuation from a meaningful pattern. In this sense, patience can make control more precise, because action is taken only when the evidence becomes weaker rather than stronger.

문제 3

선택지를 고른 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

정답 3번 · weaker → stronger

앞 문장들은 충분한 관찰을 통해 의미 있는 패턴이 확인될 때 행동해야 한다고 설명합니다. 따라서 evidence는 약해질 때가 아니라 강해질 때 조치해야 합니다.

PASSAGE 4

Where Should the Sentence Go?

주어진 문장: That shared structure makes later comparisons more reliable because everyone is recording the same kinds of information.

Teams often learn from projects by writing short reviews after the work is finished. ( ① ) If each person records whatever seems memorable, however, the reviews can be difficult to compare. ( ② ) One solution is to use a common template that asks about goals, decisions, unexpected events, and results. ( ③ ) The template does not make every project identical; it simply creates a stable frame for reflection. ( ④ ) Over time, repeated reviews can reveal patterns that would be hard to notice in isolated stories. ( ⑤ )

문제 4

주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳은?

선택지를 고른 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

정답 3번 · ③

That shared structure는 바로 앞의 common template을 받습니다. 뒤 문장은 template이 프로젝트를 동일하게 만드는 것이 아니라 안정적인 틀을 제공한다고 보완하므로 ③이 자연스럽습니다.

PASSAGE 5

Maps Simplify on Purpose

A useful map never includes everything that exists in the territory it represents. A subway map may ignore actual distance and surface geography in order to make connections between stations easier to see. This selective distortion is not necessarily an error. It becomes a problem only when users mistake the map’s purpose and expect it to answer a different question. Good representations therefore depend on both selection and context: they remove information to clarify a task, while making clear what kind of task they are designed to support.
