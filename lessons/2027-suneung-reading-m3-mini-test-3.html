실전 고정 5문항

마지막 세트는 풀이 순서를 바꾸지 않고 끝까지 유지하는 연습입니다. 막히는 문항은 정한 시간 안에 중단하고 마지막 검토에서 다시 돌아오세요.

5문항
PASSAGE 1

Redundancy as Insurance

Engineers often remove duplicated components to reduce cost and complexity. Yet duplication can also provide protection. If two independent sensors measure the same condition, one can reveal when the other has failed. The second sensor may seem unnecessary during normal operation precisely because the first one usually works. Its value appears only when ordinary assumptions break down. Redundancy is therefore not simply waste; in some systems it is a way of purchasing reliability before failure occurs.

PASSAGE 2

Predictability Builds Trust

Trust is often associated with good intentions, but predictability matters as well. A colleague who sometimes helps greatly and sometimes ignores basic agreements may be generous yet difficult to rely on. Stable expectations let people coordinate without constantly checking what will happen next. This does not mean behavior must never change. It means that changes should be explained in ways that preserve a usable pattern. In many relationships, trust grows not from perfect consistency but from ________.

PASSAGE 3

Where Should the Sentence Go?

주어진 문장: In other words, the value of a backup depends on whether it can fail differently from the main system.

A backup is useful only if it protects against the failures that threaten the primary system. ( ① ) Two copies stored on the same damaged device may look like redundancy but can disappear together. ( ② ) Similarly, two teams using the same flawed data may repeat the same mistake. ( ③ ) Independence matters because shared weaknesses can defeat several layers at once. ( ④ ) This is why resilient systems often diversify not only the number of backups but also their location, method, or source. ( ⑤ )

PASSAGE 4

Evidence and Explanation

① A strong explanation does more than fit the facts already known. ② It should also help us expect what would happen under new conditions. ③ If two theories explain the same past observations, a useful test is to identify a situation in which they make different predictions. ④ Scientific journals vary greatly in page length, citation style, and the number of articles they publish each year. ⑤ Evidence from that situation can then help distinguish between explanations that previously looked equally successful.

정답 4번 · ④

①~③과 ⑤는 설명의 가치를 새로운 상황의 예측과 검증으로 평가하는 흐름입니다. ④의 학술지 형식 정보는 논점과 무관합니다.

PASSAGE 5

Optimization Has a Horizon

A decision that is efficient in the short run may create costs later. Cutting maintenance can improve this month’s budget while increasing future breakdowns. Keeping inventory extremely low can reduce storage costs but make supply interruptions more damaging. The lesson is not that efficiency is undesirable. It is that every claim of efficiency depends on a time horizon. A choice may look optimal when only immediate costs are counted and much less attractive when delayed consequences are included.
