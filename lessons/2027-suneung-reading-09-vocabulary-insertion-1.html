2문항
PASSAGE 1

Useful Friction in Digital Design

Designers often try to remove every extra step from a digital service. Convenience matters, but a small amount of friction can sometimes improve decisions. A confirmation screen before sending money, for example, may feel inefficient, yet it gives users a chance to notice an incorrect account number. In the same way, a short delay before publishing a public post can make an impulsive response less automatic. Such pauses are valuable when the cost of an error is high. The goal is not to make systems deliberately confusing, but to place brief obstacles where they encourage reflection. Good design therefore distinguishes between friction that merely wastes time and friction that causes careless action.

문제 1

밑줄 친 낱말 중 문맥상 적절하지 않은 것은?

선택지를 고른 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

정답 5번 · causes → prevents

글은 오류 비용이 큰 상황에서 짧은 마찰이 성급한 행동을 막는 기능을 설명합니다. 따라서 마지막에는 ‘부주의한 행동을 일으키는 마찰’이 아니라 ‘부주의한 행동을 막는 마찰’이 되어야 합니다.

PASSAGE 2

Where Should the Sentence Go?

주어진 문장: That advantage, however, disappears when people stop checking whether the stored answer still fits the present situation.

External memory has clear benefits. ( ① ) A calendar can store dates we would otherwise have to remember, and a navigation app can keep track of a route while we focus on traffic. ( ② ) By moving stable information outside the mind, such tools reduce mental load. ( ③ ) This allows attention to be used for problems that change from moment to moment. ( ④ ) A saved route may become poor advice after a road closes, just as an old note may no longer match a revised plan. ( ⑤ ) Effective use of external memory therefore requires not only storing information but also knowing when to update it.

문제 2

주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳은?

선택지를 고른 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

정답 1번 · ④

주어진 문장의 That advantage는 바로 앞의 ‘주의를 변화하는 문제에 쓸 수 있다’는 장점을 받고, however 뒤에서는 그 장점의 한계를 제시합니다. 이어지는 폐쇄 도로와 오래된 메모가 그 한계를 구체화하므로 ④가 가장 자연스럽습니다.
